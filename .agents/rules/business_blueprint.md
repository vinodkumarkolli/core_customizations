# Business Blueprint & Rule Registry

This document serves as the single source of truth for all domain logic and operational business rules implemented in the `core_customizations` app. All Python, JS, and JSON Fixture changes must strictly map back to one of the following Business Rules `[BR-XXX]`.

## Epic 1: Dual Sales & Fulfillment Architecture
* **`[BR-SALES-001]` Wholesale Dispatch-First Flow**: For B2B/Wholesale transactions, a `Delivery Note` is strictly required before a `Sales Invoice` can be created. The Delivery Note is responsible for all stock ledger movements and batch allocations. The subsequent Sales Invoice must not update stock (`update_stock = 0`).
* **`[BR-SALES-002]` Retail POS Counter Flow**: For Over-The-Counter transactions, the `POS Invoice` handles immediate stock and payment. The end-of-day POS Closing Entry creates a consolidated `Sales Invoice` with `update_stock = 1`, completely bypassing the Delivery Note requirement.

## Epic 2: Sales Returns & Credit Notes
* **`[BR-RET-001]` Wholesale Return Flow**: Stock returns must be processed via a `Delivery Note` with `is_return = 1` (reverting stock), while financial refunds are handled via a separate `Sales Invoice` Credit Note with `is_return = 1` and `update_stock = 0`. Credit Notes bypass the forward Delivery Note requirement.
* **`[BR-RET-002]` POS Return Flow**: Immediate POS refunds are handled by a `POS Invoice` with `is_return = 1`, which instantly restocks the godown and refunds the customer before shift consolidation.

## Epic 3: 3PL Logistics & Transporter Management
* **`[BR-LOG-001]` Customer Transporter Defaults**: When creating a Delivery Note, Transporter, Origin Hub, and Destination Godown must auto-populate from the Customer's primary defaults.
* **`[BR-LOG-002]` Sync LR Details**: Transporter Lorry Receipt (LR) Details, Vehicle No, and Receipt Image captured on the Delivery Note must synchronize strictly across both the Delivery Note and linked Sales Invoice.

## Epic 4: Indian GST E-Way Bill Compliance
* **`[BR-EWB-001]` Single E-Way Bill Rule**: Only a single E-Way Bill must be generated (against the Tax Invoice) for the entire transit.
* **`[BR-EWB-002]` 50km First-Mile Auto Exemption (Rule 138(3))**: When stock moves from 3PL Godown to Transporter Hub within 50km via Auto/Feeder, Part A is mandatory but Part B (Vehicle No) is legally exempt.
* **`[BR-EWB-003]` Consignor Part B Authority**: If the transporter fails to update Part B for the line-haul truck, the 3PL dispatcher retains the legal authority and responsibility to update Part B directly from the ERPNext portal using the issued LR number.

## Epic 5: Inventory Guardrails
* **`[BR-INV-001]` Single Warehouse Confinement**: A single `Delivery Note` is strictly confined to dispatching goods from one single warehouse. Mixed warehouse dispatches on the same document are prohibited and must trigger validation errors.

## Epic 6: Packing Slips & Carton Management
* **`[BR-PAC-001]` Draft Carton Packing Slips**: Generating a packing slip creates it in a `Draft` state linked to specific items. Draft packing slips must be excluded from the final Delivery Note print format.
* **`[BR-PAC-002]` Bulk Submission Synchronization**: Submitting a Delivery Note must automatically submit all attached `Draft` packing slips. Cancelling a Delivery Note must auto-cancel all submitted packing slips.

## Epic 7: Document Dependency & Cancellation Guardrails
* **`[BR-CAN-001]` Strict Reverse Cancellation Sequence**: Cancellation must strictly follow downstream reversal: `Payment Entry` -> `Sales Invoice` -> `Delivery Note` -> `Sales Order`. Attempting to cancel an upstream document with an active downstream dependency must be blocked by a `LinkValidationError`.

## Epic 8: Purchase & Inbound Logistics
* **`[BR-PUR-001]` 3PL Inbound Receipt Dependency**: When a Purchase Invoice contains any 3PL Item (custom_3pl_item = 1), it must not update stock directly. Every 3PL Item row must be billed against a linked Purchase Receipt to ensure the 3PL warehouse handles the actual inbound stock movement.

## Epic 9: Automated Procurement (MR → PO Automation)
* **`[BR-PROC-001]` Supplier Source of Truth**: The authoritative source for an Item's supplier is the **"Item Supplier"** child table on the Item master (Purchasing tab), not Item Default. All automation logic must query `tabItem Supplier` for supplier resolution.
* **`[BR-PROC-002]` Draft PO Generation**: All auto-generated Purchase Orders must remain in **Draft** state (`docstatus = 0`) for mandatory human review and approval before submission. The system must never auto-submit a PO.
* **`[BR-PROC-003]` Single-Supplier Constraint**: The MR → PO automation is **only triggered when every item on the Material Request has exactly one (1) supplier** configured in its Item Supplier table. If any item has zero or more than one supplier, the automation must abort and log a descriptive error message, requiring the purchasing team to create the PO manually.
* **`[BR-PROC-004]` Warehouse Propagation**: The `set_warehouse` from the originating Material Request must be explicitly carried over to the auto-generated Purchase Order so that the shipping address resolves to the correct Warehouse delivery address on the `GST Purchase Order` print format.
* **`[BR-PROC-005]` Email Notification on PO Creation**: Upon successful draft PO creation, email notifications must be dispatched to both (a) the Supplier's primary contact email and (b) the Company's purchasing email. If neither is found, the notification falls back to the System Manager.
* **`[BR-PROC-006]` Idempotency Guard**: If a Purchase Order already exists that references the originating Material Request (checked via `Purchase Order Item.material_request`), the automation must detect this and silently exit without creating a duplicate PO.

## Epic 10: Telephony & Support Call Integration (Sarvam AI)
* **`[BR-TEL-001]` Support Ticket Call Resolution**: When a telephony call (via the CRM call integrations, extended for Sarvam AI outbound voice-agent calls) resolves to a Contact with no open `CRM Lead`/`CRM Deal`, but that Contact has an open `Issue` (Support module, status in Open/Replied/On Hold), the call must resolve to that `Issue` instead of a bare Contact, so agents reviewing the call see the ticket context.
* **`[BR-TEL-002]` Sarvam Integration Visibility**: The call-integration status API must report whether the Sarvam AI outbound voice-agent integration is configured (via the "Sarvam Outbound Settings" section's `enabled` field on the `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings` Single DocType - renamed from "Sarvam Outbound Settings" on 2026-11-02, since that doctype now also holds the independent Call Logging Settings section described in [BR-TEL-004]) alongside the existing Twilio/Exotel flags, so the frontend can surface Sarvam as an available calling medium. Sarvam outbound calling itself works against any doctype registered in `SARVAM_CALL_CONTEXT_RESOLVERS` (`Issue`, `CRM Lead`, `CRM Deal`) - extending to a new doctype means adding a resolver there and a matching Client Script button, not a new whitelisted method.
* **`[BR-TEL-003]` Sarvam Outbound Calling is Human-to-Human, Restricted to Registered Agents**: The Sarvam voice agent does not converse with the customer. It dials a registered `CRM Telephony Agent`'s mobile number first - the triggering user's own by default, or a different registered agent if one is picked via the "Call As" field offered in the calling dialog (e.g. a dispatcher routing the call to whichever teammate is actually free). Before briefing them, it asks whoever answers to read back a 4-digit verification code - generated per call and shown to the user who clicked "Call via Sarvam" in a popup - so both the person who triggered the call and the person answering it confirm they are talking about the same intended call, not voicemail or a stranger who picked up. Only on a correct match does the agent brief them on the record (Issue, CRM Lead or CRM Deal) and immediately blind-transfer the live call to the customer - so the customer's phone only rings once the resolved agent is already live on the line, and they speak directly with no AI greeting on the customer's side. Triggering this call at all - regardless of who ends up being dialed - still requires the triggering user to themselves already be registered in `CRM Telephony Agent` (the same gate CRM's own Twilio/Exotel `make_a_call` uses); a user with no `CRM Telephony Agent` record must be refused with a clear error, not silently skipped. Picking a different agent to dial only changes who gets called, never who may trigger the call.
* **`[BR-TEL-004]` Call Recording/Transcript Archival to S3**: When the "Call Logging Settings" section of `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings` is enabled (`enable_call_logging`), every Sarvam voice agent call's recording and transcript must be uploaded to S3, keyed by Sarvam's `interaction_id`, so they outlive Sarvam's own hosted URLs. This is independent of outbound calling's own `enabled` flag - call logging can be on or off regardless of it - and covers both inbound ([BR-TEL-005]) and outbound calls via a shared `upload_conversation_to_s3(direction, ...)` function. Outbound calls are stored flat under `s3_outbound_path_prefix/<interaction_id>`; calls placed as part of a Sarvam campaign are nested one level deeper as `s3_outbound_path_prefix/<campaign_id>/<interaction_id>`. Inbound calls use `s3_inbound_path_prefix` the same way. A call-logging failure (bad credentials, network issue) must never prevent the CRM Call Log from being updated - it is logged via Error Log and swallowed, not raised.
* **`[BR-TEL-005]` Inbound Call Logging**: An inbound call to a Sarvam-deployed agent gets exactly one `CRM Call Log`, written by `handle_inbound_webhook` from Sarvam's call-end webhook via `voice_agent.record_inbound_call`, keyed by `interaction_id` (`CRM Call Log` is autonamed `field:id`, so a redelivered webhook updates the same record instead of duplicating it); the webhook answers 200 for new, repeated and unusable payloads alike once the key is right. The log is type Incoming, from the caller's number as +91 and ten digits (a SHA-256 hash resolved as `caller_gate.check` does; blank or unmatched left empty; a non-Indian number kept as received), to the number they called, with the payload's start/end time and duration, the summary and transcript as FCRM Notes, and the callback Tasks `save_request` made during the call in its Links. It references the caller's CRM Lead, else Contact, else Customer - never creating one, and none for a caller not on record. Its status is decided from the number, never from the call's length or end reason: a number on the `Blocked Contact` list is `Blocked` ("on blocked list", referencing their Customer else the Blocked Contact); a non-Indian number is `Blocked` ("non-Indian number"); otherwise `No Answer` when the caller never spoke, else `Completed` (reason "transferred" when the agent's `transferred` output variable says so) - a blank or unmatched number is not a block. The reason is kept in `custom_status_reason`. No Lead, Deal or Task is created here. The recording/transcript archival is [BR-TEL-004]'s shared helper, queued after the commit with `direction = "inbound"`.
* **`[BR-TEL-006]` Live Transfer Target Is Decided On The Server**: Whether the inbound voice agent may transfer a live call to a person, and to whom, is decided by `voice_agent.transfer_target(phone, executive, interaction_id)` - not by the agent calling an outside time service (timeapi.io) or placeholder routing endpoints (httpbin). The open hours (10:00-20:00), days (Monday-Saturday), time zone (Asia/Kolkata), "callers on record only", the executives (each with a number, CRM user, On Break tick and spoken Aliases), Sticky Agent (off) and Busy Timeout (5 minutes) all come from the "Live Transfer" section of `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings`, never from code or the prompt. After the gates (`outside_hours`, `unknown_caller`): an executive the caller named (by name or alias, enabled only) gets the call unless busy or on break (`executive_busy`; unknown name `executive_not_found`); with no name and Sticky Agent on, the executive who last took this caller's transferred call, else their lead / account owner, if free; otherwise round robin - the next free executive in table order after the one on the newest routed attempt; nobody free is `all_busy`. Every decision is a `Sarvam Transfer Attempt` row with its reasons; an executive is busy while On Break or while they have a Routed/Connected attempt younger than the timeout. Checking and routing hold a row lock on the executives and write the Routed row in the same transaction, so two callers can never be routed to one executive. The agent gets the call's `interaction_id` by passing it to `caller_gate.check` at call start, which echoes it back (Sarvam tools read system variables only at call start), and sends it with each `transfer_target`; the call-end webhook ([BR-TEL-005]) closes the attempt by `interaction_id` only, idempotently: transferred -> Connected (the Call Log's receiver is the executive; they stay busy until the timeout, since Sarvam's webhook arrives at hand-over, not when the executive's call ends), not transferred -> Not connected (free at once); an attempt with no call end expires after the timeout. Any internal error answers open false with reason `error` (the agent takes a callback); only the busy check fails open. Every allowed answer carries `hours_text`, the hours and days as a spoken sentence built from the settings ("10 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday"), so the agent never says hours from its prompt. Managers see who is busy and can clear the busy flags from the Settings form.
* **`[BR-TEL-007]` Inbound Call Lifecycle Is Webhook-Driven, One Call Log Per Interaction**: An inbound voice-agent call is recorded by two Sarvam webhooks, not by the agent's own tool calls: `voice_agent.call_started` (call start) creates the `CRM Call Log` keyed by Sarvam's `interaction_id` (status "In Progress", referencing the caller's CRM Lead, else Contact) and returns the caller context the agent starts the conversation with; `sarvam_telephony.handle_inbound_webhook` (call end, [BR-TEL-005]) completes that same record - `CRM Call Log` is autonamed `field:id`, so both webhooks and any redelivery of either always land on one record. The call-end webhook also stores the agent's `output_agent_variables` on the Call Log, its conversation ref, the call summary as an `FCRM Note` linked from the Call Log, the transcript (`interaction_transcript`) as one `FCRM Note` referencing the Call Log (updated, not duplicated, on redelivery - not a Comment), and any `preferences` object onto the caller's record ([BR-CRM-007]); for a blocked/foreign caller ([BR-CRM-002]) only the raw variables are kept. The agent no longer creates or updates Call Logs itself (its former `create_call_log` tool created a second, differently-named log alongside the webhook's).

## Epic 11: CRM Lead & Contact Data Integrity
* **`[BR-CRM-001]` One CRM Lead Per Mobile Number**: Creating a `CRM Lead` whose (normalized) `mobile_no` matches an existing Lead's must be refused with `frappe.DuplicateEntryError`, naming the conflicting Lead. This is a server-side `validate` hook - not a check any one caller is responsible for - so it holds regardless of what creates the Lead (Desk UI, Data Import, the CRM frontend, or an external integration such as the Sarvam voice agent's own lead-creation tool, which was observed creating duplicate Leads for repeat callers). It runs after `phone_normalise.normalise_lead`'s `before_validate` hook, so two spellings of the same number are recognized as the same number before being compared.
* **`[BR-CRM-002]` Caller Gate - Blocked/Foreign Caller Lockout**: The Sarvam "Sravi Enterprises Customer Care" inbound agent acts against this CRM entirely as one integration user, named by `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings.caller_gate_automation_user` (not hardcoded, so it can be repointed or the gate disabled by leaving it blank, without a code change) - whether the call turns out to be from a blocked number, a foreign number, or a normal Indian caller. Prompt-level checks (an on-start webhook against a blocked-numbers list, a regex gate on the caller's number) were found not to reliably stop the model from reading or writing CRM data anyway, so this is enforced on the server instead, scoped to that one configured user so a staff member's own Desk/CRM-frontend session is never affected: a `validate` hook on `CRM Lead`, `Contact`, `Customer`, `CRM Deal`, `Quotation` and `FCRM Note` refuses a create/update whose resolved phone number is blocked or foreign with a fixed `CALLER_BLOCKED: reason=<BLOCKED_LIST|FOREIGN_NUMBER>; mobile_no=<...>` token (same convention as [BR-CRM-001]'s `DUPLICATE_LEAD:` token), and matching `permission_query_conditions`/`has_permission` hooks keep that same user from reading such a record via a list, search or direct fetch. The `Blocked Contact` DocType (mobile number normalised to its last 10 digits, `enabled` flag) is the single source of truth, replacing the Blocked Contacts Google Sheet; `core_customizations.core_customizations.caller_gate.check(phone)` is the whitelisted method Sarvam's on-start webhook (or any other caller-gate check) calls instead of that sheet's Apps Script. `check` also echoes the call's `interaction_id` back unchanged ([BR-TEL-006]), and returns `phone`: a real number as +91 and ten digits, or - for a 64-character SHA-256 hash of the E.164 number - the number on a CRM Lead, Contact, Customer or enabled Blocked Contact that hashes to it (the gate then judges that number, not the hash); blank, unmatched, foreign or refused callers get `""`.
* **`[BR-CRM-003]` Blocked Contact Escalation Approval Process**: Only `System Manager` may create a `Blocked Contact` directly - a `Sales User` instead raises a `Blocked Contact Request` from the "Escalate for Blocking" button on the CRM Lead form (snapshotting that Lead's own `lead_name`/`mobile_no` and a mandatory `reason` at escalation time, not tracking the Lead live), which is immediately submitted into the native Frappe `Workflow` "Blocked Contact Escalation" (states Draft -> Pending -> Approved/Rejected, reusing the same global Workflow State/Action Master records the pre-existing "Timesheet Approval" workflow already uses, rather than inventing new ones, so only `System Manager` can action a Pending request). A request reaching `Approved` is what actually creates (or re-enables an existing, disabled) `[BR-CRM-002]` `Blocked Contact` for that number - server-side, via `caller_gate.handle_blocked_contact_request_update`, not a side effect any UI step is trusted to perform. Rejecting a request that had already been Approved (the workflow allows reversing a decision) disables that `Blocked Contact` again rather than deleting it, via `caller_gate.handle_blocked_contact_request_cancel`. One open (Draft/Pending) escalation per Lead at a time is enforced in `Blocked Contact Request.validate`.
* **`[BR-CRM-004]` One Call Saves The Caller's Whole Request**: `voice_agent.save_request` saves everything a caller asked for in one all-or-nothing transaction, as the Caller Gate Automation User so [BR-CRM-001]/[BR-CRM-002]'s guards still apply. A **new caller** (no CRM Lead, Contact-linked Customer or Customer for their last 10 digits) gets a `CRM Lead` (`lead_owner` from settings, category in `custom_caller_category`, store type in `custom_store_type`, and the address, area, requirement and WhatsApp number as `Label: value` lines in `company_description` - the `Address:` line is what [quotation_gst] reads the pincode from) plus a callback `CRM Task`, and, when they asked for items and their category is quotable ([BR-CRM-005]), a `CRM Deal` and draft Quotation ([BR-CRM-006]). A caller who **already has a CRM Lead** never gets a second one: the result is `status: "duplicate"` with the existing Lead's id and the [BR-CRM-001] `DUPLICATE_LEAD:` token - creating a new lead is deferred to the existing one - and the request itself is recorded on a callback Task against that Lead. An **existing Customer** gets a `CRM Deal` (their open Deal is reused, never duplicated - found by the caller's Contact on the Deal's Contacts table, else their number; a new Deal is created with that Contact as its primary contact, since CRM Deal blanks `mobile_no` without one, so `upsert_quote` followed by `save_request` is one Deal and one Quotation) and, for items, a draft Quotation; with no items, or when the quote can't be made, a callback Task. Callback Tasks are assigned to `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings.callback_task_assignee` at its configured priority, and one open Task per record per day is updated rather than duplicated, so a repeated save in the same call changes nothing. When Sarvam gives no caller number, the number the caller says is accepted as `callback_number` - only when the line's `phone` is blank (otherwise ignored), only an Indian mobile (+91 and ten digits, else `CALLBACK_NUMBER_INVALID`), through the same caller gate - and saved as a callback only: a Lead (same duplicate rule) and a callback Task whose notes start "Callback request (number given by the caller, not the line):", never a Quotation or a Deal.
* **`[BR-CRM-005]` Caller Category Maps To A Customer Group On The Server**: The agent passes the category the caller chose on the call as `caller_type` - `individual`, `retailer`, `wholesale` or `distributor` - and the server maps it via `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings > Caller Categories`: individual -> Individual (quoted at Standard Selling); wholesale and distributor -> Commercial (not quoted by the agent; the team calls back); retailer -> one of the retail groups (Chennai GT, Chennai Pharma, Coimbatore Customers, MT Category D, Pondichery Customers), which depends on area and store type and so has no default row - such a lead is saved with a `CUSTOMER_GROUP_NEEDS_REVIEW` flag on its callback Task for the team to assign. An unrecognised category is saved as given with an `UNKNOWN_CATEGORY` flag. Both flags apply only to a caller with no Customer, or whose Customer's group has no Caller Category row - an existing Customer with a mapped group is already classified, whatever category the caller says. For an existing Customer, their own Customer Group is what counts, and the agent is told whether that group is one it serves (`customer.group_supported`).
* **`[BR-CRM-006]` The Agent Reads Quote Figures, It Never Computes Them**: `voice_agent.upsert_quote` creates (or, the same day, updates) the caller's draft `Quotation` and returns that saved document's own net total (before any charges), GST lines (CGST+SGST in-state / IGST out-of-state, rates from the items' HSN-driven Item Tax Templates - e.g. HSN 30049011 -> GST 5%), shipping (from the Shipping Rule) and rounded total, so the figures spoken on the call are by construction the figures on the document. It also returns `gst_percent` and `gst_amount` - the GST rows' rates and (already rounded) amounts summed - so the agent says one generic "GST" figure and never IGST/CGST/SGST or a sum of its own. An existing Customer is quoted as the Customer at the first price list found on the Customer itself, then its Customer Group, then the Caller Categories fallback for its category - never a guessed default; none found is `NO_PRICE_LIST`, and a chosen list that is disabled, not for selling or in another currency is `PRICE_LIST_UNUSABLE`, never silently replaced; a caller with only a CRM Lead is quoted through a `CRM Deal` (ERPNext Quotations accept `CRM Deal`, never a `CRM Lead`, and never an ERPNext `Lead`) at their category's price list. Items listed in `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings > Pack Items` - today the Sastry Balm 50 INR packs of 4, 10 and 20 - are sold only to that row's Customer Group (Individual) and always carry that row's Shipping Rule (Next Day Shipping, by net weight); asking for one for any other group is refused (`PACK_NOT_AVAILABLE`). A quote is refused rather than quoted wrong when GST can't be determined (no delivery pincode on record: `PINCODE_REQUIRED`), an item has no price in the caller's price list (`PRICE_NOT_FOUND`), or items ship under different rules (`MIXED_SHIPPING`); a refused quote leaves nothing behind.
* **`[BR-CRM-007]` Caller Preferences Are Structured Data On The Caller's Record**: A caller's voice-agent preferences (e.g. `preferred_language`, `preferred_flow`) are a JSON object in `custom_caller_preferences` on their CRM Lead, else on their Contact (a Customer-only caller has no Lead), read by `voice_agent.get_caller_preferences` / returned by `caller_context`, and written by `voice_agent.set_caller_preferences` (merged by default) or by the call-end webhook ([BR-TEL-007]) - not free-text comments the agent has to parse.
* **`[BR-CRM-008]` Saved Addresses Are Offered, Reused And Follow The Lead To Its Customer**: `caller_context` (and the call-started webhook, from the same function) returns the caller's saved `Address` records - those linked through Dynamic Link to their Customer if they have one, otherwise to their CRM Lead; never parsed from the Lead's free-text `company_description` - enabled only, shipping-preferred first, then primary, then most recently modified, capped at `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings.address_list_limit` (5). `upsert_quote` and `save_request` accept one of those as `address_name`, which wins over a spoken address or pincode and becomes the Quotation's `shipping_address_name`; an `address_name` that isn't the caller's own enabled Address is refused with `ADDRESS_NOT_ON_RECORD` and nothing is saved. For a Customer, India Compliance decides the place of supply from it exactly as in Desk - which address it reads follows Accounts Settings > "Determine Address Tax Category From" (currently "Billing Address", so GST follows the Customer's billing address whatever shipping address is chosen); for a CRM Deal quotation, which India Compliance does not read addresses for, the Address's pincode drives the same in-state/out-state decision as [BR-CRM-006]. A caller with no Customer who gives an address (line, city, pincode; state from the pincode if not given) has it saved as a Shipping `Address` linked to their CRM Lead - the same Dynamic Link mechanism that links a Lead's Contact - reused when the same line and pincode come again, alongside the existing `Address:` line in `company_description`; a missing city or pincode saves the request without the Address, flagged `ADDRESS_NOT_SAVED`. When the Lead later becomes a Customer, its Addresses are linked to that Customer too, keeping the Lead link (`lead_address_link`, on Customer and Contact `on_update`): neither Lead -> Deal conversion nor the CRM integration's Deal -> Customer step carries them across on its own. The Lead is found from the Customer's `crm_deal`, a Contact linked to both, or else the single CRM Lead on the Customer's mobile number - never guessed among several: when a step finds more than one Lead, nothing is linked and the Customer is logged once to Error Log ("Lead Address Link: could not decide the CRM Lead for Customer ...") for the team to link by hand.
* **`[BR-CRM-009]` Waiting Callbacks Are Counted On A Desk Workspace**: The "Callbacks" Desk workspace (Sales User, Sales Manager, System Manager) shows two Number Cards - "Callbacks Awaiting" and "Overdue Callbacks" - from `callbacks_dashboard.callbacks_awaiting` / `overdue_callbacks`. They count only voice-agent callback Tasks: CRM Tasks titled "Callback: ..." ([BR-CRM-004]) owned by the Caller Gate Automation User from `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings` (the title alone when that is blank) - never Tasks staff create by hand - whose status is Backlog, Todo or In Progress; overdue additionally means a start date before today, so a callback moved to a later date is not overdue. Clicking a card opens the CRM Task Report View with exactly the filters that produced the number. Counts respect the signed-in user's CRM Task permissions. The Frappe CRM dashboard is not used for this: its cards cannot be clicked through without changing the CRM app.
* **`[BR-CRM-010]` Voice-Agent Calls Are Counted By How They Ended**: The "Callbacks" Desk workspace also shows this month's inbound voice-agent calls - CRM Call Logs of type Incoming with telephony medium Sarvam, as written by [BR-TEL-005] - in three Number Cards from `callbacks_dashboard`: "Live Agent Answered" (status Completed, Status Reason "transferred"), "Resolved by Sarvam" (status Completed, no Status Reason) and "Blocked Calls" (status Blocked). Missed calls (No Answer) are in none of them. Each card opens the CRM Call Log Report View with exactly its filters, and counts respect the signed-in user's Call Log permissions. "Live Agent Answered" depends on the agent setting its `transferred` output variable when it hands a call over.
