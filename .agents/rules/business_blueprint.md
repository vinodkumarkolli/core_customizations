# Business Blueprint & Rule Registry

This document serves as the single source of truth for all domain logic and operational business rules implemented in the `core_customizations` app. All Python, JS, and JSON Fixture changes must strictly map back to one of the following Business Rules `[BR-XXX]`.

## Epic 1: Dual Sales & Fulfillment Architecture
* **`[BR-SALES-001]` Wholesale Dispatch-First Flow**: For B2B/Wholesale transactions, a `Delivery Note` is strictly required before a `Sales Invoice` can be created. The Delivery Note is responsible for all stock ledger movements and batch allocations. The subsequent Sales Invoice must not update stock (`update_stock = 0`).
* **`[BR-SALES-002]` Retail POS Counter Flow**: For Over-The-Counter transactions, the `POS Invoice` handles immediate stock and payment. The end-of-day POS Closing Entry creates a consolidated `Sales Invoice` with `update_stock = 1`, completely bypassing the Delivery Note requirement.

## Epic 2: Sales Returns & Credit Notes
* **`[BR-RET-001]` Wholesale Return Flow**: Stock returns must be processed via a `Delivery Note` with `is_return = 1` (reverting stock), while financial refunds are handled via a separate `Sales Invoice` Credit Note with `is_return = 1` and `update_stock = 0`. Credit Notes bypass the forward Delivery Note requirement.
* **`[BR-RET-002]` POS Return Flow**: Immediate POS refunds are handled by a `POS Invoice` with `is_return = 1`, which instantly restocks the godown and refunds the customer before shift consolidation.

## Epic 3: 3PL Logistics & Transporter Management
* **`[BR-LOG-001]` Customer Transporter Defaults**: When creating a Delivery Note, Transporter, Origin Hub, and Destination Godown must auto-populate from the Customer's primary defaults.
* **`[BR-LOG-002]` Sync LR Details**: Transporter Lorry Receipt (LR) Details, Vehicle No, and Receipt Image captured on the Delivery Note must synchronize strictly across both the Delivery Note and linked Sales Invoice.

## Epic 4: Indian GST E-Way Bill Compliance
* **`[BR-EWB-001]` Single E-Way Bill Rule**: Only a single E-Way Bill must be generated (against the Tax Invoice) for the entire transit.
* **`[BR-EWB-002]` 50km First-Mile Auto Exemption (Rule 138(3))**: When stock moves from 3PL Godown to Transporter Hub within 50km via Auto/Feeder, Part A is mandatory but Part B (Vehicle No) is legally exempt.
* **`[BR-EWB-003]` Consignor Part B Authority**: If the transporter fails to update Part B for the line-haul truck, the 3PL dispatcher retains the legal authority and responsibility to update Part B directly from the ERPNext portal using the issued LR number.

## Epic 5: Inventory Guardrails
* **`[BR-INV-001]` Single Warehouse Confinement**: A single `Delivery Note` is strictly confined to dispatching goods from one single warehouse. Mixed warehouse dispatches on the same document are prohibited and must trigger validation errors.

## Epic 6: Packing Slips & Carton Management
* **`[BR-PAC-001]` Draft Carton Packing Slips**: Generating a packing slip creates it in a `Draft` state linked to specific items. Draft packing slips must be excluded from the final Delivery Note print format.
* **`[BR-PAC-002]` Bulk Submission Synchronization**: Submitting a Delivery Note must automatically submit all attached `Draft` packing slips. Cancelling a Delivery Note must auto-cancel all submitted packing slips.

## Epic 7: Document Dependency & Cancellation Guardrails
* **`[BR-CAN-001]` Strict Reverse Cancellation Sequence**: Cancellation must strictly follow downstream reversal: `Payment Entry` -> `Sales Invoice` -> `Delivery Note` -> `Sales Order`. Attempting to cancel an upstream document with an active downstream dependency must be blocked by a `LinkValidationError`.

## Epic 8: Purchase & Inbound Logistics
* **`[BR-PUR-001]` 3PL Inbound Receipt Dependency**: When a Purchase Invoice contains any 3PL Item (custom_3pl_item = 1), it must not update stock directly. Every 3PL Item row must be billed against a linked Purchase Receipt to ensure the 3PL warehouse handles the actual inbound stock movement.

## Epic 9: Automated Procurement (MR → PO Automation)
* **`[BR-PROC-001]` Supplier Source of Truth**: The authoritative source for an Item's supplier is the **"Item Supplier"** child table on the Item master (Purchasing tab), not Item Default. All automation logic must query `tabItem Supplier` for supplier resolution.
* **`[BR-PROC-002]` Draft PO Generation**: All auto-generated Purchase Orders must remain in **Draft** state (`docstatus = 0`) for mandatory human review and approval before submission. The system must never auto-submit a PO.
* **`[BR-PROC-003]` Single-Supplier Constraint**: The MR → PO automation is **only triggered when every item on the Material Request has exactly one (1) supplier** configured in its Item Supplier table. If any item has zero or more than one supplier, the automation must abort and log a descriptive error message, requiring the purchasing team to create the PO manually.
* **`[BR-PROC-004]` Warehouse Propagation**: The `set_warehouse` from the originating Material Request must be explicitly carried over to the auto-generated Purchase Order so that the shipping address resolves to the correct Warehouse delivery address on the `GST Purchase Order` print format.
* **`[BR-PROC-005]` Email Notification on PO Creation**: Upon successful draft PO creation, email notifications must be dispatched to both (a) the Supplier's primary contact email and (b) the Company's purchasing email. If neither is found, the notification falls back to the System Manager.
* **`[BR-PROC-006]` Idempotency Guard**: If a Purchase Order already exists that references the originating Material Request (checked via `Purchase Order Item.material_request`), the automation must detect this and silently exit without creating a duplicate PO.

## Epic 10: Telephony & Support Call Integration (Sarvam AI)
* **`[BR-TEL-001]` Support Ticket Call Resolution**: When a telephony call (via the CRM call integrations, extended for Sarvam AI outbound voice-agent calls) resolves to a Contact with no open `CRM Lead`/`CRM Deal`, but that Contact has an open `Issue` (Support module, status in Open/Replied/On Hold), the call must resolve to that `Issue` instead of a bare Contact, so agents reviewing the call see the ticket context.
* **`[BR-TEL-002]` Sarvam Integration Visibility**: The call-integration status API must report whether the Sarvam AI outbound voice-agent integration is configured (via the "Sarvam Outbound Settings" section's `enabled` field on the `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings` Single DocType - renamed from "Sarvam Outbound Settings" on 2026-11-02, since that doctype now also holds the independent Call Logging Settings section described in [BR-TEL-004]) alongside the existing Twilio/Exotel flags, so the frontend can surface Sarvam as an available calling medium. Sarvam outbound calling itself works against any doctype registered in `SARVAM_CALL_CONTEXT_RESOLVERS` (`Issue`, `CRM Lead`, `CRM Deal`) - extending to a new doctype means adding a resolver there and a matching Client Script button, not a new whitelisted method.
* **`[BR-TEL-003]` Sarvam Outbound Calling is Human-to-Human, Restricted to Registered Agents**: The Sarvam voice agent does not converse with the customer. It dials a registered `CRM Telephony Agent`'s mobile number first - the triggering user's own by default, or a different registered agent if one is picked via the "Call As" field offered in the calling dialog (e.g. a dispatcher routing the call to whichever teammate is actually free). Before briefing them, it asks whoever answers to read back a 4-digit verification code - generated per call and shown to the user who clicked "Call via Sarvam" in a popup - so both the person who triggered the call and the person answering it confirm they are talking about the same intended call, not voicemail or a stranger who picked up. Only on a correct match does the agent brief them on the record (Issue, CRM Lead or CRM Deal) and immediately blind-transfer the live call to the customer - so the customer's phone only rings once the resolved agent is already live on the line, and they speak directly with no AI greeting on the customer's side. Triggering this call at all - regardless of who ends up being dialed - still requires the triggering user to themselves already be registered in `CRM Telephony Agent` (the same gate CRM's own Twilio/Exotel `make_a_call` uses); a user with no `CRM Telephony Agent` record must be refused with a clear error, not silently skipped. Picking a different agent to dial only changes who gets called, never who may trigger the call.
* **`[BR-TEL-004]` Call Recording/Transcript Archival to S3**: When the "Call Logging Settings" section of `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings` is enabled (`enable_call_logging`), every Sarvam voice agent call's recording and transcript must be uploaded to S3, keyed by Sarvam's `interaction_id`, so they outlive Sarvam's own hosted URLs. This is independent of outbound calling's own `enabled` flag - call logging can be on or off regardless of it - and covers both inbound ([BR-TEL-005]) and outbound calls via a shared `upload_conversation_to_s3(direction, ...)` function. Outbound calls are stored flat under `s3_outbound_path_prefix/<interaction_id>`; calls placed as part of a Sarvam campaign are nested one level deeper as `s3_outbound_path_prefix/<campaign_id>/<interaction_id>`. Inbound calls use `s3_inbound_path_prefix` the same way. A call-logging failure (bad credentials, network issue) must never prevent the CRM Call Log from being updated - it is logged via Error Log and swallowed, not raised.
* **`[BR-TEL-005]` Inbound Call Logging**: An inbound call to a Sarvam-deployed agent has no pre-existing `CRM Call Log` (unlike outbound, where `trigger_sarvam_call` creates one before dialing) - `handle_inbound_webhook` creates it from Sarvam's call-end webhook, keyed by `interaction_id` (`CRM Call Log` is autonamed `field:id`, so a redelivered webhook updates the same record instead of duplicating it). The caller's number is resolved to a reference record using the same `get_contact_lead_or_deal_from_number` priority (CRM Lead > CRM Deal > Issue > Contact) as [BR-TEL-001], so inbound and outbound calls land on records consistently. An inbound call connects by definition and is never retried by Sarvam, so every such Call Log is created with `status = "Completed"` - there is no status to map, unlike the outbound webhook's `SARVAM_STATUS_MAP`. The recording/transcript archival itself is [BR-TEL-004]'s shared helper, called with `direction = "inbound"`.

## Epic 11: CRM Lead & Contact Data Integrity
* **`[BR-CRM-001]` One CRM Lead Per Mobile Number**: Creating a `CRM Lead` whose (normalized) `mobile_no` matches an existing Lead's must be refused with `frappe.DuplicateEntryError`, naming the conflicting Lead. This is a server-side `validate` hook - not a check any one caller is responsible for - so it holds regardless of what creates the Lead (Desk UI, Data Import, the CRM frontend, or an external integration such as the Sarvam voice agent's own lead-creation tool, which was observed creating duplicate Leads for repeat callers). It runs after `phone_normalise.normalise_lead`'s `before_validate` hook, so two spellings of the same number are recognized as the same number before being compared.
* **`[BR-CRM-002]` Caller Gate - Blocked/Foreign Caller Lockout**: The Sarvam "Sravi Enterprises Customer Care" inbound agent acts against this CRM entirely as one integration user, named by `Sarvam Voice Agent Settings.caller_gate_automation_user` (not hardcoded, so it can be repointed or the gate disabled by leaving it blank, without a code change) - whether the call turns out to be from a blocked number, a foreign number, or a normal Indian caller. Prompt-level checks (an on-start webhook against a blocked-numbers list, a regex gate on the caller's number) were found not to reliably stop the model from reading or writing CRM data anyway, so this is enforced on the server instead, scoped to that one configured user so a staff member's own Desk/CRM-frontend session is never affected: a `validate` hook on `CRM Lead`, `Contact`, `Customer`, `CRM Deal`, `Quotation` and `FCRM Note` refuses a create/update whose resolved phone number is blocked or foreign with a fixed `CALLER_BLOCKED: reason=<BLOCKED_LIST|FOREIGN_NUMBER>; mobile_no=<...>` token (same convention as [BR-CRM-001]'s `DUPLICATE_LEAD:` token), and matching `permission_query_conditions`/`has_permission` hooks keep that same user from reading such a record via a list, search or direct fetch. The `Blocked Contact` DocType (mobile number normalised to its last 10 digits, `enabled` flag) is the single source of truth, replacing the Blocked Contacts Google Sheet; `core_customizations.core_customizations.caller_gate.check(phone)` is the whitelisted method Sarvam's on-start webhook (or any other caller-gate check) calls instead of that sheet's Apps Script.
* **`[BR-CRM-003]` Blocked Contact Escalation Approval Process**: Only `System Manager` may create a `Blocked Contact` directly - a `Sales User` instead raises a `Blocked Contact Request` from the "Escalate for Blocking" button on the CRM Lead form (snapshotting that Lead's own `lead_name`/`mobile_no` and a mandatory `reason` at escalation time, not tracking the Lead live), which is immediately submitted into the native Frappe `Workflow` "Blocked Contact Escalation" (states Draft -> Pending -> Approved/Rejected, reusing the same global Workflow State/Action Master records the pre-existing "Timesheet Approval" workflow already uses, rather than inventing new ones, so only `System Manager` can action a Pending request). A request reaching `Approved` is what actually creates (or re-enables an existing, disabled) `[BR-CRM-002]` `Blocked Contact` for that number - server-side, via `caller_gate.handle_blocked_contact_request_update`, not a side effect any UI step is trusted to perform. Rejecting a request that had already been Approved (the workflow allows reversing a decision) disables that `Blocked Contact` again rather than deleting it, via `caller_gate.handle_blocked_contact_request_cancel`. One open (Draft/Pending) escalation per Lead at a time is enforced in `Blocked Contact Request.validate`.
